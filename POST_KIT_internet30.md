## Posting tips (important for reach)
1. **Time:** post the same video on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube within the same hour. Best is between 19:00 and 22:00 your time.
2. **First 60 minutes:** reply to every comment with a question back (e.g. "why OFF? 👀"). Replies double the comment count and tell the algorithm people are talking.
3. **Pin** the first comment.
4. **Don't delete and repost** if the views are slow in the first hours. Wait 48 h.
5. **Keep posting daily** in the same "What if…" niche for 2 weeks so the algorithm learns who your audience is.
6. **Part 2:** after 2–3 days, count ON vs OFF in the comments. Tell me the winner and I'll make Part 2.
