## The logic of the story
Every step follows from the one before it:
1. Internet off: **5 billion** people offline.
2. **Hour 1:** no messages, videos or social media.
3. **Hour 3:** card payments need the network, so cards stop and shops go **cash only**.
4. **Day 1:** everyone needs cash, so bank lines go around the block.
5. **Day 3:** no online orders and no deliveries, so shelves empty.
6. The stock market can't trade without the network, so it closes and the world loses billions every hour.
7. **Day 7:** people go outside, talk to neighbors and read books, and radio is the only news.
8. **Day 14:** screen time 0, more sleep.
9. **The downside:** hospitals, airports and banks run on paper, so everything is slow.
10. **The choice:**
    - **ON:** life as usual comes back (money, job, everything).
    - **OFF:** 30 more days of real life, but nothing works.

A real trade-off with no "right" answer, so people argue in the comments.
