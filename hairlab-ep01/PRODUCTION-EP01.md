**Scientific accuracy:**
- The script uses "يقدر" (can) and "كيختالف" (varies) everywhere, never "always".

## 6. Music
- Light lo-fi pop at 96 BPM.
- Chords Am – F – C – G: pad, plucked arpeggio, soft kick and hi-hat.
- **Ducking:** the music drops automatically by about 7 dB whenever someone speaks.
- **Teaser (0:58.8–1:04.4):** only a dark drone remains, for suspense, then the beat comes back with the ending.
- 2-second fade-out at the end.
- Final loudness: −15 LUFS (good for Instagram and TikTok).

## 7. Transitions
- **Water wipe:** a blue circle fills the screen in 0.32 s, then opens onto the next scene in 0.5 s. Used for scenes 1→2, 2→3, 3→4 and 4→5.
- **5→6:** same room; the cards swap with a bounce.
- **6→7:** the lights come back after the teaser.
- **7 → logo card:** fade.
- Every scene has a slow camera push-in (1.00 → 1.05).
- In scene 2, the camera pans down from the sky to underground.
- In scene 4, the lens shrinks to make room for the comparison.

## 9. Animation prompts (for an AI video tool, if you want to remake a scene)
Paste this description at the start of every prompt so the characters stay the same:

> **CHARACTERS (keep identical):** (A) Young Moroccan male cartoon presenter. Big rounded head and large brown eyes with white highlights. Thick dark-brown eyebrows. Dark-brown tousled hair with one curl on the forehead. Teal open shirt (#23A0A0) over a white T-shirt. Flat 2D vector style with no outlines on the clothes. (B) Small blue water-drop mascot with a glossy gradient, big cartoon eyes, pink cheeks and little white gloves. **STYLE:** flat 2D motion graphics in a dark purple room (#2C1260) with warm yellow lamp light, plants and an arched window. Accents in yellow (#FFD23F) and water blue (#4FC3F7). Vertical 9:16, smooth easing, no text distortion.

1. **Hook:** Medium close-up of the presenter. His eyebrows rise in surprise as he speaks to the camera and raises an open palm. A golden magnifier pops in beside him, showing a hair strand with a few small white mineral specks. The water drop bounces in from the right, waves, and says "it might be me!". Slow push-in camera.
2. **Mineral origin:** Rain falls from soft clouds onto purple mountains. The camera tilts down through a cross-section of the ground: brown soil, then cream limestone blocks. A glowing blue water stream winds down through the rock. Yellow "Ca" and silver "Mg" orbs detach from the rock and join the stream. The water drop rides the stream happily.
3. **Hard water:** Underwater view with light rays and rising bubbles. Glossy yellow "Ca²⁺" and silver "Mg²⁺" ions float, and their number increases. A horizontal gauge needle moves from "soft" to "hard". Then three water glasses in a row with 2, 5 and 10 mineral dots, each labeled.
4. **Hair under the microscope:** A golden-ringed microscope lens over a brown hair strand with visible cuticle scales. A few small white crystal deposits appear one by one along the cuticle edges (sparse, not a crust) while a shower counter rises. The lens shrinks; below it, two cards compare a clean shiny strand with a duller strand that has a few specks.
5. **Clarification:** In the room, the presenter is serious and raises an index finger. A card shows dandruff flakes on the scalp ≠ mineral crystals on a hair strand. The card flips to a water-hardness test: a strip dips into a glass, its pads change color, and a result scale appears. The presenter points at it.
6. **Care and teaser:** The water drop scratches its head and looks confused. A checklist card animates in with two green checkmarks. The lights dim and a spotlight reveals a silhouetted shampoo bottle with a big yellow question mark and sparkles. Mysterious mood.
7. **Ending:** The presenter waves with the drop beside him. A follow card shows a yellow "Follow" button that gets tapped by a hand and turns green. Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Facebook icons pop in. The scene fades to the HairLab logo (golden hair strand + blue drop in a circle) on purple.

## 10. Changing something
1. To change text or a scene: edit `ep01.html` (open it with `?t=12` to see the frame at 12 seconds).
2. To use a new voice: replace `audio/host.wav` and `audio/drop.wav` and the word timings, then run `python3 build_audio.py`.
3. Rebuild the music and SFX: `python3 build_mix.py`.
4. Render the frames with `render2.js`, then join them with ffmpeg (the settings are in the commit message).
