**Scientific accuracy:**
- The script says "يقدر" (can) and "كيختالف" (varies), never "always".
- It doesn't link calcaire to hair loss.
- It separates dandruff from calcaire.
- It invents no numbers.

## 4. Sound effects
- **Start:** whoosh and impact on the title, plus water drops from the shower.
- **"ترسبات":** a pop on the word.
- **Qtira's entrance:** a boing.
- **Scene changes:** whoosh and splash (0:08 · 0:18 · 0:28 · 0:41).
- **Scene 2:** rain, an underground rumble, and flowing water bubbles.
- **Labels and keywords:** a pop on each label, and a thump on "القاصح".
- **Scene 4:** 14 small clicks as the deposits appear.
- **Checklist:** two dings.
- **Summary:** a pop and a ding on each of its 4 lines.
- **Follow button:** a click and a success sound.
- **Logo:** a shimmer.

## 5. Music
- Light lo-fi at 96 BPM, with chords Am – F – C – G.
- **Ducking:** the music drops automatically by about 7 dB whenever someone speaks.
- Fade-out at the end.
- Final loudness: −15 LUFS.

## 6. Transitions
- **Water wipe** at 0:08, 0:18, 0:28 and 0:41.
- **Cards** swap with a bounce.
- **Camera:** a slow push-in in every scene, and a pan down to underground in scene 2.

Paste this description at the start of every prompt:

Vertical 9:16, smooth easing. **MASCOT:** a small blue glossy water-drop character with big cartoon eyes, pink cheeks and little white gloves. Keep it identical in every shot. 4. A microscope lens over a brown hair strand with cuticle scales. A few sparse white crystals appear (not a crust). Then a comparison of a clean shiny strand vs a duller strand with a few specks.
5. A card: dandruff flakes on the scalp ≠ mineral crystals on hair. Then a test strip dips into a glass and its pads change color.

7. A follow card whose button gets tapped and turns green, social icons, the drop waving, then the white "naqiva" wordmark on purple.

## 8. Changing something
1. To change text or a scene: edit `ep01.html` (open it with `?t=12` to see the frame at 12 seconds).
2. To change the voice: replace the files in `audio/` and the word timings, then run `python3 build_audio.py`.
3. Rebuild the music and SFX: `python3 build_mix.py`.
4. Render the frames with `render2.js`, then join them with ffmpeg.
