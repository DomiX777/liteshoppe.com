# Next video ideas (saved)

Each one uses the same formula as the 3M-view reference video:
- A "What if… YOU" hook in the first 2 seconds.
- A big number that changes every few seconds.
- Escalation, then a twist in the middle.
- A countdown near the end.
- A 2-button choice CTA at the end.

## 1. What if you could see everyone's last day? ✅ (in production)
- **Premise:** numbers above people's heads show how many days they have left. The hero saves lives.
- **Twist:** every life he saves costs days from his own number.
- **Choice:** **SAVE / WALK AWAY**.

## 2. What if you got $1 every time someone said your name?
- **Escalation:** the money counter climbs $1 → $40,000,000 (school, a stadium chant, then the internet).
- **Twist:** someone with your exact name goes viral and starts "stealing" your dollars.
- **Choice:** **CHANGE NAME / KEEP IT**.

## 3. What if time froze for everyone except you?
- **Escalation:** Day 1, then Day 100, then Day 1,000 alone in a frozen world (free food, empty roads, total silence).
- **Twist:** one day, something else moves: a person walking toward you.
- **Choice:** **FOLLOW HIM / HIDE**.
